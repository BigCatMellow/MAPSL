## Scope and acceptance

Use the Pilot THINK testing protocol and existing MAPSL research as the
method baseline. Compare named upstream mechanisms against current MAPSL
call sites and immutable fixtures when available. Distinguish source
inspection, upstream reported results, reproduced MAPSL behavior and new
hypotheses. Use existing owners and finish tests.

- Each area gives concrete mechanisms, primary sources, MAPSL fit, risks,
  and a keep, adapt, test or defer disposition.
- Propose a bounded comparison against current behavior, with frozen inputs,
  negative controls, a success threshold, a cost measure and stopping criteria.
- Reproduce local baselines where available. Any candidate effect awaits the
  corresponding comparison. Avoid another policy, state store or work queue.
- Publish the integrated research under the existing research router after
  independent evidence and scope review, then verify the exact draft and CI.

## Boundaries

Public source inspection, existing local tests and harmless disposable
fixtures are in scope. Candidate implementation, production settings, paid
or external task runs, policy changes, protected merges and cutover retain
their separate approval boundaries. Review defensive authority mechanisms
through local policy decisions and synthetic records.
